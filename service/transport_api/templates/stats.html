AI clients

{{ window_control("ai", "AI clients") }}

Requests from clients that say they are an AI or run inside one: crawlers that read for training and search, assistants fetching for a person (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity …), apps running in the Claude desktop app or built in Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable and the like (recognised by the page they call from), and agents and MCP servers that name themselves. A script an AI wrote for someone looks like any other script and is not counted here. Recorded since 24. September 2026, the weeks before the service counted them itself filled in from the access logs.

What is asked for

{{ window_control("places", "what is asked for") }}

Counted exactly as the clients wrote them. A station can be asked for by its name or by its number, the Swiss stop ID (8507000 is Bern), and the count does not look either up: «Bern», «bern» and «8507000» are three entries for the same station, and a station may therefore rank lower than it is asked for. Rate-limited and failed requests are not counted.

Calls and errors

Errors are answers the API could not give: search.ch failing or timing out, unknown paths, wrong methods, invalid parameters. Rate-limited requests (HTTP 429) are not errors and not counted here. Errors are counted from 22. November 2015 on.

Each bar is the calls of a year or a day, the red part its errors. Hover a bar for the figures and the error rate. The paler bar is the year or day so far; the rate for 2015 counts from 22. November on, and there is none before.
